# Recert support

- **Documentation**: this site and the in-app help text.
- **Questions and bug reports**: email <support@recert.dev>, or open an issue at
  <https://github.com/ezekiel06/recert-site/issues>.
- **Security issues**: email <security@recert.dev>, or use GitHub's private vulnerability
  reporting on the same repository ("Security" tab → "Report a vulnerability"). Never a public issue.
- **Hours and response target**: Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 US Central; first response
  within two business days.
