# Recert — Access Reviews for Jira and Confluence

Recert is an Atlassian Forge app that runs the periodic user-access reviews SOC 2 and
ISO 27001 auditors ask for. It works out who effectively has access to each Jira project
and Confluence space and why, routes each review to the person who owns that project or
space, records keep / revoke / exception decisions with a reason and a timestamp, and
exports the evidence.
